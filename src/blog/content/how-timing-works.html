The short version

Every timed role has a minimum, a target and a maximum. The timer turns green at the minimum, yellow at the target and red at the maximum. That is it. Everything else is detail.

The three cards

CardShown atMeaning
Green5:00Minimum reached. You are in the time window.
Yellow6:00Target reached. Start wrapping up.
Red7:00Maximum reached. Finish now.

The table shows a 5 to 7 minute prepared speech. The timer does the same for every role, with that role's own numbers.

Qualifying and grace

A speaker qualifies when their time falls between the minimum and the maximum. The report in this app also allows a 30 second grace on each side, so it can tell the difference between a speaker who was a few seconds off and one who was well off.

  • Qualified: from the minimum to the maximum.
  • Grace: up to 30 seconds under the minimum or over the maximum.
  • Under or over: beyond the grace.

Table Topics is the one exception: it has grace over the maximum but none under the minimum.

Round Robin is a special case. It shows only a red card at the maximum, because the point is a quick turn for each person.

These are the presets built into the timer. Clubs differ, and Pathways projects have their own times, so always check your club's rules.

Who is the Timer?

The Timer is a role at the meeting. They start the clock when each speaker begins, show the cards and write down the times. In an online meeting the Timer shares the timer screen or puts it on camera.
