What the role is

A speech evaluator listens to one prepared speaker and gives feedback in front of the club. Good evaluations name what worked, offer one or two things to try next, and end on encouragement.

The evaluation is a speech in its own right, with its own time limit, so it gets its own timer.

How it is timed

An evaluation is usually 2 to 3 minutes. The Evaluator and Prepared Speech Evaluator presets both use 2:00 / 2:30 / 3:00.

CardShown atMeaning
Green2:00Minimum reached. You are in the time window.
Yellow2:30Target reached. Start wrapping up.
Red3:00Maximum reached. Finish now.

The card colours come from three numbers: the green card at the minimum, the yellow card at the target and the red card at the maximum. Before the minimum the screen stays plain.

In the report, a speaker qualifies between the minimum and the maximum. There is a 30 second grace on each side, so a time up to 30 seconds under the minimum or over the maximum is marked as grace. Beyond that it is marked as under or over time.

Set it up in the timer

  1. Sign in and open your meeting (or create one from the home page).
  2. Press Add Speaker.
  3. Type the person's name and Prepared Speech Evaluator as the role.
  4. In Event Type choose Prepared Speech Evaluator. The three card times fill in for you (2:00 / 2:30 / 3:00).
  5. Press Save. The role appears in the roster, in the order you added it. Use the order box to move it.
  6. When the role starts, press Start Timer on that row, then START. Press STOP when they finish. The time is saved to the roster.

What it looks like during the role

The green card at 2:00 tells the evaluator they have said enough to qualify. Yellow at 2:30 means one more point and a closing line.

Tips

  • Name the role "Prepared Speech Evaluator" or "Evaluator". The report groups roles with "evaluator" in the name together.
  • Add the evaluator right after the speaker they evaluate, so the roster reads in meeting order.
  • If the evaluator is a new member, tell them the numbers beforehand.

Limits differ between clubs and between Pathways projects. These are the defaults built into the timer. If your club uses other numbers, change the three times on the form before you save.
