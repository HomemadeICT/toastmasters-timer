What the role is

The Ice Breaker is a member's first prepared speech. The speaker introduces themselves to the club, so it is personal and often nervous.

Because it is a first speech, the timer's job is to be calm and clear rather than strict.

How it is timed

An Ice Breaker is 4 to 6 minutes. The Ice Breaker preset is 4:00 for green, 5:00 for yellow and 6:00 for red.

CardShown atMeaning
Green4:00Minimum reached. You are in the time window.
Yellow5:00Target reached. Start wrapping up.
Red6:00Maximum reached. Finish now.

The card colours come from three numbers: the green card at the minimum, the yellow card at the target and the red card at the maximum. Before the minimum the screen stays plain.

In the report, a speaker qualifies between the minimum and the maximum. There is a 30 second grace on each side, so a time up to 30 seconds under the minimum or over the maximum is marked as grace. Beyond that it is marked as under or over time.

Set it up in the timer

  1. Sign in and open your meeting (or create one from the home page).
  2. Press Add Speaker.
  3. Type the person's name and Ice Breaker as the role.
  4. In Event Type choose Ice Breaker. The three card times fill in for you (4:00 / 5:00 / 6:00).
  5. Press Save. The role appears in the roster, in the order you added it. Use the order box to move it.
  6. When the role starts, press Start Timer on that row, then START. Press STOP when they finish. The time is saved to the roster.

What it looks like during the role

Green appears at 4:00, which tells the speaker they have reached the minimum. Yellow at 5:00 is a gentle hint that the end is near.

Tips

  • Meet the speaker before the meeting and show them what the cards look like.
  • Do not rush to red. A first speaker who sees red may freeze, so keep it steady.
  • Save the time and tell them afterwards. It is a nice number to look back on.

Limits differ between clubs and between Pathways projects. These are the defaults built into the timer. If your club uses other numbers, change the three times on the form before you save.
