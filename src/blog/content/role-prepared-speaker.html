What the role is

A prepared speaker delivers a speech they have planned and practised, usually a project from their Pathways path. It is the centre of the meeting, so the timing matters to the speaker, to the evaluator and to everyone who follows.

The speaker does not time themselves. The meeting's Timer holds up the cards or, in an online meeting, shows them on screen.

How it is timed

A standard prepared speech runs 5 to 7 minutes. The timer's Prepared Speech preset uses exactly that.

CardShown atMeaning
Green5:00Minimum reached. You are in the time window.
Yellow6:00Target reached. Start wrapping up.
Red7:00Maximum reached. Finish now.

The card colours come from three numbers: the green card at the minimum, the yellow card at the target and the red card at the maximum. Before the minimum the screen stays plain.

In the report, a speaker qualifies between the minimum and the maximum. There is a 30 second grace on each side, so a time up to 30 seconds under the minimum or over the maximum is marked as grace. Beyond that it is marked as under or over time.

Set it up in the timer

  1. Sign in and open your meeting (or create one from the home page).
  2. Press Add Speaker.
  3. Type the person's name and Prepared Speech as the role.
  4. In Event Type choose Prepared Speech. The three card times fill in for you (5:00 / 6:00 / 7:00).
  5. Press Save. The role appears in the roster, in the order you added it. Use the order box to move it.
  6. When the role starts, press Start Timer on that row, then START. Press STOP when they finish. The time is saved to the roster.

What it looks like during the role

At 5:00 the screen turns green, at 6:00 yellow and at 7:00 red. The big number counts up so the speaker can glance at it too.

Tips

  • Check your Pathways project for its time. Some later projects are longer than 5 to 7 minutes, and the timer has presets for several of them.
  • Tell the speaker where the cards will appear before they start, so they are not surprised.
  • Press STOP the moment the speaker finishes their last sentence, not when the applause ends.

Limits differ between clubs and between Pathways projects. These are the defaults built into the timer. If your club uses other numbers, change the three times on the form before you save.
