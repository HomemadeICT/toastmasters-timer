What the role is

Table Topics is impromptu speaking. The Table Topics Master asks a question and a member answers on the spot, with no preparation.

Many members answer in a few seconds and stop. The green card at one minute gives them a goal to aim for.

How it is timed

A Table Topics answer is 1 to 2 minutes. The preset is 1:00 / 1:30 / 2:00.

CardShown atMeaning
Green1:00Minimum reached. You are in the time window.
Yellow1:30Target reached. Start wrapping up.
Red2:00Maximum reached. Finish now.

The card colours come from three numbers: the green card at the minimum, the yellow card at the target and the red card at the maximum. Before the minimum the screen stays plain.

In the report, the maximum has a 30 second grace. Table Topics has no grace on the short side: finishing under the minimum is marked as under time.

Set it up in the timer

  1. Sign in and open your meeting (or create one from the home page).
  2. Press Add Speaker.
  3. Type the person's name and Table Topic as the role.
  4. In Event Type choose Table Topic. The three card times fill in for you (1:00 / 1:30 / 2:00).
  5. Press Save. The role appears in the roster, in the order you added it. Use the order box to move it.
  6. When the role starts, press Start Timer on that row, then START. Press STOP when they finish. The time is saved to the roster.

What it looks like during the role

Each speaker gets a fresh timer. After the answer, press STOP, go back to the roster with EXIT, and start the next speaker.

Tips

  • You will usually not know the names ahead of time. Add speakers as they stand up, or add placeholders such as "Table Topic Speaker 01" in advance.
  • Use NEXT to jump straight to the next speaker in the roster without going back to the list.
  • One minute is a short time to fill. Do not show red early. Wait for the real maximum.

Limits differ between clubs and between Pathways projects. These are the defaults built into the timer. If your club uses other numbers, change the three times on the form before you save.
