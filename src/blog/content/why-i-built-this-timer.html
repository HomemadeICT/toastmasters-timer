Why a timer

I time the meetings at my Toastmasters club. A live meeting is a busy place. The agenda changes, speakers swap places, and the Timer has to show a clear signal to the whole room without losing focus.

Paper timing sheets are easy to get wrong. A general stopwatch app does not know what a Table Topics speaker or an Evaluator is. I wanted something that knew the roles and showed a colour you can read across a room.

What I built

  • A full-screen timer that turns green, yellow and red at each role's limits.
  • A roster of speakers you can reorder when the agenda changes.
  • Presets for the usual meeting roles, and custom times for anything else.
  • A printable A4 report that shows who qualified.
  • Two small helpers for online meetings: a zoom control for sizing the display and an OBS shift for camera layouts.

How it is made

It is a PHP and MySQL web app, packaged in Docker. Each person has their own account and sees only their own meetings.
